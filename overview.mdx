The **Overview** tab is the main place to configure Helply’s core automation and routing for a server.

This is where you decide where Helply should listen, which roles count as staff, how quickly Helply should reply, and where automation logs should go.

---

## What the Overview tab controls

### Ticket destinations

Choose the ticket channels or categories where Helply should listen for new tickets.

Once selected, Helply watches those destinations for support activity.

### Support team roles

Choose the roles that count as your support team.

Messages from these roles will not trigger Helply responses, which helps Helply stay out of the way when human staff are already handling the conversation.

### Support team handoff

Enable **Support team handoff** if you want Helply to automatically pause when a support team member speaks.

This helps create a smoother handoff from AI to human support and prevents Helply from continuing to reply once a staff member has stepped in.

### Server information

Add a short summary of your server so Helply has better context when responding.

This is a simple way to give Helply background about your community, product, or support environment.

<Note>
  If Helply has trouble understanding your server's meaning, adding server information is important.
</Note>

### Response delay

Set how long Helply waits before replying.

This gives humans a chance to respond first and helps you control how aggressive or reserved Helply should feel in support channels.

### Respond on ticket opening

Choose whether Helply should respond to the initial ticket opening message.

When this is enabled, Helply can reply immediately when a ticket is created, before the user sends a follow-up message.

### Log channel

Choose the channel where Helply sends logs, summaries, and automation events.

This gives your team a central place to monitor what Helply is doing.

### Bot Customization

This lets you customize how Helply appears in your server.

<Info>
  Uploaded images for bot customization must be under 1MB.
</Info>

<Note>
  Bot customization is a Pro-only feature.
</Note>
