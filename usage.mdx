The **Usage** tab shows how many Helply credits your server has used during the current billing period.

Use this tab to track monthly credit usage, see how many credits remain, check when limits reset, and review usage activity across Helply’s agents.

## What the Usage tab does

  </Card>
  <Card icon="coins" title="Monitor remaining credits">

## Monthly credits

At the top of the page, Helply shows your current monthly credit usage for the server.

This includes:

- total credits used
- total credits remaining
- percent of plan used
- time remaining until reset

This gives you a quick view of how close the server is to its monthly limit.

## Monthly reset cycle

Credits reset on a monthly cycle.

Credits are rounded to the nearest whole number, and limits reset on the **last day of each month**.

<Note>
  Usage totals and remaining credits always reflect the current monthly credit period for that server.
</Note>

## Monthly usage log

The **Monthly usage log** shows usage activity for the current credit period.

You can expand this section to see the credits used by agent. This helps you understand where usage is coming from and which parts of Helply are consuming the most credits.

The log resets automatically with your monthly credit period.

<Info>
  Every file search tool call uses **50 Helply credits**.
</Info>

Read more about lowering credit usage in [Intelligence](/intelligence) and [Optimize](/optimize).
