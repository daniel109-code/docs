Helply is an AI support agent for Discord communities. It helps your team handle support faster by answering questions inside tickets, threads, and channels using your server’s own context.

<Tip>
  Helply is designed to feel like a real support teammate. It can answer questions, ask clarifying follow-ups, and take selected support actions based on your configuration.
</Tip>

## What Helply is built for

Helply works best in servers that need fast, consistent support at scale.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card icon="temperature-high" title="High-volume support">
    Great for communities that receive frequent repeat questions and want faster first responses.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="ticket" title="Ticket and forum workflows">
    Built for servers that run support through tickets, forum posts, threads, or dedicated help channels.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="chevrons-down" title="Fewer staff interruptions">
    Reduces unnecessary pings by handling common questions and routing the right issues to humans.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="brain" title="Server-specific answers">
    Keeps answers aligned with your own docs, rules, terminology, and support process.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What Helply does day to day

Helply can support your team across the full flow of a conversation.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card icon="block-question" title="Answer questions">
    Respond to support questions directly inside Discord tickets and support forum threads.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="database" title="Use your context">
    Pull from your configured sources, including documentation, files, and websites.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="route" title="Guide users">
    Suggest next steps and walk users through common fixes or troubleshooting flows.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="arrow-up-right" title="Escalate when needed">
    Hand issues off to staff when a request needs human judgment, review, or access.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="paperclip" title="Keep support organized">
    Help wrap up completed conversations by marking forum posts as resolved or closing finished threads and tickets when enabled.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How Helply stays accurate

Helply relies on the information and rules you provide. That keeps replies grounded in how your server actually works.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="files" title="Server-specific documentation and context">
    Helply uses the sources you connect, such as docs, uploaded files, and websites, to answer with relevant server knowledge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="user-shield" title="Configured behavior rules and limits">
    You control how Helply responds, what tone it uses, where it can reply, and what boundaries it should follow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="shield-check" title="Approved actions and escalation paths">
    Helply only performs the actions and escalation flows you enable, so it stays aligned with your support workflow.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  This setup helps Helply stay consistent with your server’s terminology, policies, and workflows.
</Note>
