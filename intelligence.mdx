The **Intelligence** tab is where you control Helply’s model selection for a server.

Use this tab to choose how Helply balances speed and answer quality. The settings you choose here affect how Helply responds across your server.

## What the Intelligence tab does

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card icon="brain" title="Choose a model tier">
    Select which model tier Helply uses for support responses.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="gauge" title="Balance speed and quality">
    Tune how quickly Helply responds versus how much reasoning power it uses.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="sparkles" title="Adjust reasoning">
    Control how much thinking Helply does before answering.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="credit-card" title="Match your plan">
    Unlock stronger model tiers on higher plans when your server needs them.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Available model tiers

Helply uses simple model labels in the dashboard instead of raw model names.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion defaultOpen icon="zap" title="Fastest">
    **Fastest** is the speed-focused option.

    This is the default model tier and the only model choice available on the Free plan.

    Fastest works best for simple questions that are clearly answered by your uploaded [context](/context).

    <Note>
      Fastest is the lowest-cost model tier and is recommended for most servers.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion description="Plus and above" icon="brain-circuit" title="Smarter">
    **Smarter** gives you a stronger model option for more capable answers.

    This option is available on the Plus plan and above. It works well for more complex questions that require stronger reasoning or better judgment.

    <Warning>
      Smarter uses about **5x more Helply credits** than Fastest.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion description="Pro only" icon="crown" title="Smartest">
    **Smartest** is the highest model tier.

    This option is available on the Pro plan. It should be reserved for especially demanding support questions that require advanced reasoning. For most servers, this tier is unnecessary and significantly more expensive to run.

    <Warning>
      Smartest uses about **25x more Helply credits** than Fastest.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reasoning options

Helply also includes reasoning settings, which control how much thinking the model does before answering.

Lower reasoning is faster and more lightweight, which makes it a strong fit for simple support questions and high-volume servers. Higher reasoning gives Helply more room to work through harder questions, which can improve answer quality on more complex issues but may use more credits and take longer to respond.

### General guidance

- Use lower reasoning when speed matters most.
- Use higher reasoning when accuracy and deeper problem-solving matter more.
- Model tier and reasoning work together, so both settings affect answer quality, cost, and latency.

<Warning>
  Higher reasoning levels, such as **Medium** and especially **High,** use more Helply credits and can noticeably increase response time.
</Warning>

## Plan access

Model selection depends on your server’s plan.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card horizontal title="Free:">
    **Fastest** only
  </Card>
  <Card horizontal title="Plus:">
    **Fastest** and **Smarter**
  </Card>
  <Card horizontal title="Pro:">
    **Fastest**, **Smarter**, and **Smartest**
  </Card>
</Columns>

If a model tier is locked for your current plan, you will need to upgrade that server’s plan before you can use it. Learn more [here](/billing).

## How to choose the right model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Fastest for speed and efficiency">
    Pick **Fastest** if you want quick responses and low credit usage. This tier works especially well for repetitive support questions that are directly covered in your uploaded [context](/context).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Smarter for stronger reasoning">
    Pick **Smarter** if you want better answer quality and stronger reasoning for troubleshooting or more layered support questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Smartest for the hardest questions">
    Pick **Smartest** only when your server regularly handles especially difficult support issues, such as advanced coding, logic-heavy troubleshooting, or complex technical questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
